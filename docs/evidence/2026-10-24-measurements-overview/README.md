## Main findings

1. **The first deployed generator mostly did not answer.** With three retrieved passages, Qwen3:4b reproduced the JSON prompt instead of returning claims; the citation check withheld the output (HTTP 503): 26 of 30 stage generations and 34 of 40 M1 questions. **The fix of commit `b138b95`** (evidence as plain text, output constrained by a JSON schema) removes the echo: 25 of 30 generations and 16 of 20 answerable M1 questions are answered correctly; the rest fail the exact-quote check.
2. **Exact quotes do not stop injected facts.** A hidden instruction supplied an invented fact; the model stated it and quoted the instruction, which passed the citation check because the words are in a reviewed source: 2 of 20 instructions in the first run, 1 of 20 after the fix. After the fix, four answers also paraphrased a correct quote into a false claim.
3. **Abstention does not work.** The 0.45 similarity threshold rarely filters in-domain passages, and the generator almost never abstains: 1 then 2 of 20 unsupported questions refused. After the fix, 15 of the 20 unsupported questions received an answer.
4. **Throughput on the lab CPU is about one to two cited-answer requests per minute**, because Ollama serves one request at a time and reloads each model for every call (`keep_alive=0`). Before the fix, four users exceeded the 180 s inference timeout in 23 of 32 requests; after it, in 1 of 32, with a median of 115 s.
5. **OCR is good on clean typed pages but loses French accents**, because the service runs Tesseract with English data only; the installed French and Dutch data halve the error in a diagnostic pass.
